Our Story

Africa's Trusted Partner for Premium Edible Oils

AMC GHANA is the West African subsidiary of AMC Polska — a Polish full-service export company with decades of experience supplying premium sunflower oils and fats to global markets. We bring European quality directly to Ghana and the wider African continent.

Our products meet the strictest European food safety standards, and we work with leading retailers, manufacturers, and distributors across more than 40 countries. From commodity bulk supply to custom private-label packaging, AMC GHANA is your end-to-end edible oils partner.

Our Products

Our Product Range

From crude to fully refined, we supply a comprehensive range of edible oils and fats. All products are available in bulk tanker, IBC, drum, and consumer packaging formats.

Bestseller

RBD Sunflower Oil

Refined, Bleached & Deodorised sunflower oil — crystal clear, neutral taste, and high smoke point. Perfect for frying, baking, and general cooking.

Bulk / IBC ISO 22000 Non-GMO

Crude Sunflower Oil

First-press crude sunflower oil for industrial refining and processing. Available in bulk tanker shipments at competitive export pricing.

Bulk Tanker Industrial Export Grade

Hydrogenated Vegetable Fat

Fully and partially hydrogenated fats for bakery, confectionery, and margarine manufacture. Custom iodine value to specification.

Bakery Grade Custom IV Drum / Bulk

RBD Palm Olein

High-quality refined palm olein for frying applications. Excellent oxidative stability with low free fatty acid content, ideal for the food industry.

Frying Grade Low FFA IBC / Drum

RBD Corn Oil

Refined corn (maize) oil with a light colour and mild taste. Rich in polyunsaturated fatty acids. Suitable for all cooking methods.

Light Colour PUFA-Rich Consumer / Bulk

RBD Coconut Oil

Refined coconut oil for food manufacturing, cosmetics, and pharmaceutical applications. Odourless and pure white with high lauric acid content.

Food / Pharma High Lauric Drum / Tote
