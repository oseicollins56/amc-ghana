Our Story

West Africa's Trusted Sunflower Oil Partner

AMC GHANA is the West African subsidiary of AMC Polska — a Polish full-service export company with over 25 years of experience supplying premium edible oils to global markets.

We bring certified European-grade sunflower oil directly to Ghana, serving food manufacturers, distributors, retailers, and caterers across West Africa. Whether you need a full tanker or a custom private-label retail bottle, AMC GHANA is your end-to-end solution.
